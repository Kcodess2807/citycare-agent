  else may reuse or guess a session id. The design:
  - Key: storage is keyed by sha256(json([phone, session_id])), not by
    session_id alone. The second phone gets a separate, empty conversation. It

    action.
  - Why hashed: phone numbers stay out of storage keys and logs.
  - Rejected alternatives: resetting the session when the phone differs (anyone

    (leaks one patient's data to another).
